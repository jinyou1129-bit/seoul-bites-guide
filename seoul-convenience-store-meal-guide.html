Seoul Convenience Store Meal Guide

Kept in this browser only.

Published 2026-11-21

Convenience stores are useful for a quick meal between sightseeing stops. Selection varies by branch.

What to try

  • Samgak gimbap (삼각김밥): Check the filling and follow the numbered wrapper tabs.
  • Cup ramyeon (컵라면): Follow package directions and check the broth and spice level.
  • Dosirak (도시락): A boxed meal may include rice, sides and a main; read reheating instructions.
  • Banana milk (바나나우유): Check for milk and enjoy by the printed use-by date.

A simple plan

Check labels and dates, use seating only where provided, and sort waste as instructed.

Before you order

  • Confirm prices, portions and payment methods before ordering.
  • Ask about allergens and cross-contact; recipes vary by vendor.
  • Check current hours and transport schedules; availability changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are convenience-store meals vegetarian?
Not necessarily; broths, sauces and fillings can contain meat or seafood. Check ingredients.
How should I handle allergies?
Tell staff about your allergy and ask about ingredients and cross-contact. Never rely on appearance alone.
How much should I budget?
Prices vary by shop and portion. Check the displayed menu and confirm the total before ordering.
Can I pay by card?
Many businesses accept cards, but policies vary. Carry some cash as a backup.
