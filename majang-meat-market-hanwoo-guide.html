Majang Meat Market: How to Eat High-Grade Hanwoo Beef for Half Price

Buy Hanwoo at a butcher, then pay a nearby cooking restaurant to grill it. Comparing the meat price and table fee can be good value, but a half-price saving is not guaranteed: cuts, weights, grades and restaurant prices vary.

Get there

Take Seoul Subway Line 5 to Majang Station, Exit 2, and use your map for the market entrance. The market area is large; check your chosen shop and the restaurant's opening hours before you go.

Market address: 서울특별시 성동구 마장로31길 40

Butcher first, cooking restaurant second

  1. At a butcher, ask for Hanwoo (한우), the grade, cut, price per 100 g and total weight. Compare labels and prices at several counters. Ask for the full amount before the meat is cut or packed.
  2. Before buying, ask which nearby 상차림 식당 (table-setting/cooking restaurant) accepts that butcher's meat. Some are upstairs, others elsewhere; not every restaurant accepts every purchase.
  3. Ask the restaurant its per-person table charge, minimum party size, included sides, grill service and extra charges. Around ₩6,000 per person is a planning example—not a fixed market price.
  4. Take the wrapped meat and receipt to the agreed restaurant. Pay the restaurant fee separately and check the bill. Drinks, rice, soup and extra sides may cost more.
Compare like-for-like: meat price per 100 g + table fees + extras versus a restaurant menu. A lower sticker price alone does not prove a 50% saving.

Understand grades

1++ is the top Korean beef quality grade; a label may also show a marbling score. 1++ No. 9 indicates the highest marbling score within that grade. 1+ is the next quality grade down and can be less expensive. More marbling means a richer, fattier bite, not automatically a better choice for everyone. Check the actual label and price.

Three cuts to ask for

  • Salchisal (살치살): richly marbled chuck flap; buy a small portion if you like a buttery bite.
  • Kkotdeungsim (꽃등심): well-marbled ribeye; compare the per-100 g price and visible marbling.
  • Chaekkeut (채끝): striploin, often a firmer, beefier contrast to highly marbled cuts.

For two, choose a modest mixed selection and ask for the exact weight; appetites and minimums vary. Cook beef thoroughly to your preference, and use separate utensils for raw and cooked meat.

Show these phrases

한우 1+ 채끝 300그램 주세요.

Please give me 300 g of 1+ Hanwoo striploin.

100그램에 얼마예요? 총 얼마예요?

How much per 100 g? What is the total?

상차림비는 한 사람에 얼마예요?

How much is the table charge per person?
