Breakfast in Seoul: What to Eat Before Sightseeing

Kept in this browser only.

Published 2026-11-24

Breakfast can be a bakery stop, a rice-and-soup meal or something quick from a convenience store.

What to try

  • Gimbap (김밥): Rice rolls make a portable breakfast; check the filling.
  • Juk (죽): Rice porridge varieties can contain vegetables, seafood or meat.
  • Bakery bread: A neighborhood bakery is convenient for coffee and a pastry; fillings vary.
  • Gukbap (국밥): Rice with soup is hearty; ask about broth ingredients and portion size.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do all restaurants serve breakfast?
No. Some open only for lunch or dinner, so confirm current hours.
How should I handle allergies?
Tell staff about your allergy and ask about ingredients and cross-contact. Never rely on appearance alone.
How much should I budget?
Prices vary by shop and portion. Check the displayed menu and confirm the total before ordering.
Can I pay by card?
Many businesses accept cards, but policies vary. Carry some cash as a backup.
