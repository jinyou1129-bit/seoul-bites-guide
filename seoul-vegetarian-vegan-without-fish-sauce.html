Seoul Vegetarian & Vegan Food Guide: Where to Eat Without Fish Sauce

Kept in this browser only.

Published 2026-11-27

Plan meals by asking about broths, sauces and preparation.

What to try

  • Bibimbap (비빔밥): Ask about meat, egg and sauces.
  • Tofu dishes: Ask if broth contains seafood or meat.
  • Temple food (사찰음식): Confirm ingredients and suitability.
  • Vegetable gimbap (야채김밥): Check for egg, fish cake and sauces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is kimchi always vegan?
No. Kimchi can contain fish sauce or fermented seafood.
How should I handle allergies?
Tell staff about your allergy and ask about ingredients and cross-contact. Never rely on appearance alone.
How much should I budget?
Prices vary by shop and portion. Check the displayed menu and confirm the total before ordering.
Can I pay by card?
Many businesses accept cards, but policies vary. Carry some cash as a backup.
