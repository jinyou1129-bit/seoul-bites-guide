About Seoul Bites Guide

Welcome to Seoul Bites Guide! Our mission is simple: provide foreign travelers, expats, and first-time visitors with clear, no-nonsense, plain-English guides to discovering and enjoying Seoul's rich food scene.

p>

Korean dining culture is vibrant and welcoming, but navigating shared counter seating, unwritten market etiquette, and Korean-only menus can feel intimidating. We break down what to order, how much it costs, and how to eat with local confidence.

p>
