Korean Noodles in Seoul: What to Order

Kept in this browser only.

Published 2026-11-23

From warming broth to chilled noodles, Korean noodle dishes suit different seasons and appetites.

What to try

  • Kalguksu (칼국수): Hand-cut wheat noodles often come in broth that may contain anchovy, shellfish or meat.
  • Naengmyeon (냉면): Cold chewy noodles come in chilled broth or spicy sauce; ask about buckwheat.
  • Jjajangmyeon (짜장면): Wheat noodles with dark bean sauce often contain pork.
  • Japchae (잡채): Glass noodles are tossed with vegetables and sometimes meat; ask about soy and sesame.

A simple plan

Choose hot or cold noodles to match the weather and check the broth or sauce.

Before you order

  • Confirm prices, portions and payment methods before ordering.
  • Ask about allergens and cross-contact; recipes vary by vendor.
  • Check current hours and transport schedules; availability changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are noodle broths always vegetarian?
No. Anchovy, shellfish and meat broths are common, so check each dish.
How should I handle allergies?
Tell staff about your allergy and ask about ingredients and cross-contact. Never rely on appearance alone.
How much should I budget?
Prices vary by shop and portion. Check the displayed menu and confirm the total before ordering.
Can I pay by card?
Many businesses accept cards, but policies vary. Carry some cash as a backup.
