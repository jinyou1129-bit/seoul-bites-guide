Seoul Dining FAQ Accordion

Do I need to tip at Korean restaurants?

No. Tipping is not customary in Korea; some venues may add a service charge, so check the bill.

Is drinking water really free?

Yes. Drinking water is normally free, and self-service water stations are standard at many restaurants. Ask staff if unsure.

How do I find the restroom passcode?

The code is often printed on the receipt or attached to a wall-mounted key. Ask staff if you cannot find it.

Seoul Food Survival Phrasebook

식당 필수 한국어 족보 · Tap a card for a handy tip, or show the Korean to your server.

01이모님!Imo-nim!Excuse me!Tap for tip ＋

A familiar way to call a server in some casual restaurants. If unsure, use “Jeogiyo!” (저기요!).

02안 맵게 해주세요An maepge hae-juseyoPlease make it not spicy.Tap for tip ＋

Some dishes cannot be made mild; ask before ordering.

03물 좀 주세요Mul jom ju-seyoWater, please.Tap for tip ＋

Water is often self-service; look for a water station.

04이거 얼마예요?Igeo eolma-yeyo?How much is this?Tap for tip ＋

Point to the item as you ask.

05반찬 더 주세요Banchan deo ju-seyoMore side dishes, please.Tap for tip ＋

Refills are often free, but policies vary by restaurant.

06계산해 주세요Gyesan hae-juseyoCheck, please.Tap for tip ＋

In many restaurants, you pay at the counter.

TEST YOUR SEOUL IQ

1-minute Seoul food quiz

Five quick questions on ordering, markets and phrases. Miss one and we point you to the guide that fixes it.

1. Which market is famous for bindaetteok (mung-bean pancakes)?

Gwangjang Market in Jongno is the bindaetteok stop.

2. What is pork belly called on a Korean BBQ menu?

삼겹살 is pork belly; 갈비 is ribs and 차돌박이 is beef brisket.

3. How much should you tip at a Korean restaurant?

Tipping is not customary; some hotels add a service charge to the bill.

4. What does “덜 맵게 해주세요” mean?

It means “please make it less spicy” — copy it from the phrasebook above.

5. What is chimaek (치맥)?

치킨 (chicken) + 맥주 (beer). The Han River picnic guide shows how to do it.
