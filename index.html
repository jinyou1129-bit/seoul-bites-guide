Seoul Food Survival Phrasebook

식당 필수 한국어 족보 · Tap a card for a handy tip, or show the Korean to your server.

01이모님!Imo-nim!Excuse me!Tap for tip ＋

A familiar way to call a server in some casual restaurants. If unsure, use “Jeogiyo!” (저기요!).

02안 맵게 해주세요An maepge hae-juseyoPlease make it not spicy.Tap for tip ＋

Some dishes cannot be made mild; ask before ordering.

03물 좀 주세요Mul jom ju-seyoWater, please.Tap for tip ＋

Water is often self-service; look for a water station.

04이거 얼마예요?Igeo eolma-yeyo?How much is this?Tap for tip ＋

Point to the item as you ask.

05반찬 더 주세요Banchan deo ju-seyoMore side dishes, please.Tap for tip ＋

Refills are often free, but policies vary by restaurant.

06계산해 주세요Gyesan hae-juseyoCheck, please.Tap for tip ＋

In many restaurants, you pay at the counter.
