Frequently asked questions

How much should I budget for food in Seoul?

Street snacks can cost a few thousand won; noodle bowls often cost around 8,000–15,000 KRW. Barbecue and premium beef cost more. Prices vary by venue and portion.

Are these dishes spicy?

Spice levels vary by cook. Tteokbokki and many stews are commonly spicy; ask staff before ordering if you are sensitive.

Can I show Korean to restaurant staff?

Yes. Use the Show to Server button for a large Korean ordering phrase. The neighborhood copy button copies a Korean area name, not a street address.
