Korean Convenience Store Hacks: Top 6 Viral Food Combos at GS25 & CU Every Tourist Must Try

Kept in this browser only.

Turn a GS25 or CU stop into a mini food adventure. These six mixes are ideas, not fixed store menus: products, equipment, promotions and availability vary by branch.

Prices are rough planning estimates in Korean won, not verified shelf prices. Check the total in store; sizes and brands change the cost.

Use only containers labeled microwave-safe, follow each product’s heating instructions and ask before using store equipment. Handle hot water carefully. Check ingredients and cross-contact for allergies or dietary restrictions.

1. Mark Meal (마크정식)

Buy: Tteokbokki + spaghetti ramen + string cheese + sausage

Estimated total: ₩6,000–₩9,000

  1. Choose microwave-ready tteokbokki, spaghetti-style instant noodles, a ready-to-eat sausage and string cheese.
  2. Prepare the tteokbokki and noodles separately exactly as their packages direct; drain the noodles if directed.
  3. Slice the ready-to-eat sausage. In a microwave-safe bowl, combine the cooked noodles and tteokbokki, including some sauce; add sausage.
  4. Tear string cheese over the top. If the bowl and ingredients are microwave-safe, heat briefly until the cheese melts, following package guidance. Stir carefully and eat hot.

2. Fire Noodle Rice Ball Bomb

Buy: Buldak ramen + tuna mayo triangle gimbap + mozzarella cheese

Estimated total: ₩5,000–₩8,000

  1. Prepare the Buldak noodles according to the package; drain as instructed and mix in the sauce. Start with less sauce if sensitive to heat.
  2. Unwrap the tuna mayo triangle gimbap completely, including the plastic and seaweed wrapper. Break the rice ball into a microwave-safe bowl.
  3. Add the cooked noodles and top with mozzarella. Heat only if all packaging and ingredients permit microwaving; stop once the cheese melts.
  4. Mix and eat promptly. This is very spicy; dairy, fish, wheat and other allergens may be present.

3. Banana Milk Espresso Latte

Buy: Binggrae banana milk + convenience-store espresso shot + iced cup

Estimated total: ₩3,500–₩6,000

  1. Buy chilled Binggrae banana milk, an espresso shot if the branch offers one, and a cup of ice.
  2. Open the ice cup and pour in the banana milk, leaving room for coffee.
  3. Slowly add the espresso shot over the ice, stir, and taste. Use less espresso for a sweeter drink; enjoy immediately.

4. Gomtang Dumpling Soup

Buy: Instant gomtang ramen + microwaved pork mandu

Estimated total: ₩4,500–₩7,500

  1. Choose instant gomtang ramen and packaged pork mandu suitable for microwaving.
  2. Heat the mandu separately according to its label, including any venting and standing time. Make sure it is heated through.
  3. Prepare the gomtang ramen with hot water or the microwave as its own label directs.
  4. Carefully add the hot mandu to the prepared noodle soup. Let it cool enough to eat; broth and dumplings can remain very hot.

5. Sweet & Salty Pouch Drink Ice Cup

Buy: Blue lemonade or peach iced tea pouch + iced cup

Estimated total: ₩2,500–₩4,500

  1. Pick a blue lemonade or peach iced tea pouch and a separately sold ice cup; check whether a promotion bundles them.
  2. Open the ice cup, tear the pouch at the notch and slowly pour it over the ice.
  3. Stir and sip. For a sweet-and-salty contrast, enjoy alongside a separate salty snack such as crackers; it is optional and not included in the estimate.

6. Convenience Store Pocha Set

Buy: Shin Ramyun cup + cooked egg + ready-to-eat smoked chicken breast + Cass beer

Estimated total: ₩7,000–₩10,000

  1. Buy a Shin Ramyun cup, a fully cooked egg, ready-to-eat smoked chicken breast and a chilled Cass beer.
  2. Prepare the noodle cup according to its label; use hot water and wait for the stated time. Do not microwave a cup unless the label permits it.
  3. Peel the cooked egg and slice the ready-to-eat chicken with clean utensils. Add to the noodles or serve on the side; heat chicken only if its label allows.
  4. Enjoy with the beer where drinking is permitted. Alcohol sales are age-restricted; carry ID. Swap in a soft drink for an alcohol-free set.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the microwave and hot water at GS25 or CU?

Many branches provide a microwave or hot water, but equipment and seating vary. Ask staff first and follow each package’s directions.

How much do these combinations cost?

Roughly ₩2,500–₩10,000 per combination, depending on products, branch and promotions. These are planning estimates, not checked shelf prices.

Are these combinations vegetarian or allergy-safe?

Not necessarily. Noodle seasoning, sauces and fillings can contain meat, fish, dairy, egg, wheat or other allergens. Read every label and ask about cross-contact.

Can tourists buy beer?

Alcohol sales are age-restricted in Korea. Bring ID and expect an age check; choose a non-alcoholic drink if you do not drink.
