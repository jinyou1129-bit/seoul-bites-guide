Gwangjang Market Food Guide: What to Eat and How to Order

Gwangjang Market is a lively place to explore Korean street food. Browse several stalls before deciding what to try.

Five foods to try

  • Bindaetteok: savory mung-bean pancakes, often served with a soy dipping sauce.
  • Mayak gimbap: small seaweed rice rolls, often served with mustard sauce.
  • Tteokbokki: chewy rice cakes in a usually spicy red sauce.
  • Yukhoe: seasoned raw beef, often with pear and egg. Raw meat and egg carry food-safety risks; avoid if pregnant, immunocompromised or otherwise at higher risk.
  • Kalguksu: comforting knife-cut noodles in broth.

How to order

  1. Choose a stall and check the displayed price and portions.
  2. Point to the dish or menu and indicate the quantity.
  3. Confirm the price, sit where directed, and pay as requested.

Beginner tips

Bring some cash as payment methods vary, share portions to try more, and ask about ingredients if you have allergies. Expect crowds at busy times and keep walkways clear.

Frequently asked questions

Is English necessary?

Photo menus and pointing can help, though communication varies by stall.

Is all the food spicy?

No. Dishes vary; ask about sauces if you prefer mild food.

Can vegetarians eat here?

Options vary and broths or sauces may contain meat or seafood. Ask vendors about ingredients.
