Halal dining in and around Itaewon

Certification not independently verified: ask each venue for the current certifying body, certificate, scope and expiry, as well as meat sourcing, alcohol, shared equipment and cross-contact. Muslim-friendly does not automatically mean halal-certified.

  • Eid — Ask about the current halal status and Korean dishes.
  • The Halal Guys — Check whether the Seoul branch is operating and whether its local certification is current.
  • Busan Jib — Verify the exact branch, location and certification before visiting; do not assume it is in Itaewon.

Vegetarian and vegan dining

Temple food at Balwoo Gongyang

Across from Jogyesa Temple, Balwoo Gongyang offers Korean temple food. Ask about vegan options, allergens, reservations and hours. Temple food is a starting point, not a guarantee for every dietary need.

Address: 서울특별시 종로구 우정국로 56 템플스테이통합정보센터 5층

Ssam-bap and Insadong cafes

Ssam-bap (쌈밥) combines rice and leafy wraps. Ask for no meat or seafood, and check ssamjang, side dishes and broth for fish, shrimp, meat, egg and dairy. In Insadong, look for clearly labelled plant-based cafe options; ask about milk, butter, eggs, honey and shared equipment.

Ordering safely

Kimchi can contain fish sauce or salted shrimp; soup can use anchovy or meat stock. A no-meat request does not exclude seafood, dairy or eggs. Specify your needs and ask about hidden ingredients and shared utensils. For allergies, ask about cross-contact and do not eat when the answer is uncertain.

More useful phrases

육수에 고기나 생선이 들어가나요? — Does the broth contain meat or fish?

달걀과 유제품도 먹지 않아요. — I don't eat eggs or dairy either.

Frequently asked questions

Can I assume a restaurant is halal?

No. Ask for current certification, its scope and expiry, and verify kitchen practices and ingredients.

Is temple food suitable for every vegetarian?

Menus vary. Confirm stock, sauces, dairy, egg, fish and cross-contact before ordering.
