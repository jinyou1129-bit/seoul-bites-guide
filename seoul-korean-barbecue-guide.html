Korean Barbecue in Seoul: A Beginner’s Guide

Kept in this browser only.

Published 2026-11-22

Korean barbecue is a social meal built around grilling meat and sharing side dishes. Menus and minimum orders differ.

What to try

  • Samgyeopsal (삼겹살): Cook pork belly fully and use clean utensils for cooked meat.
  • Galbi (갈비): Ribs may be marinated; ask which meat is offered and about allergens.
  • Ssam (쌈): Wrap grilled meat with greens and a little sauce.
  • Banchan (반찬): Side dishes vary and can contain seafood; ask about ingredients.

A simple plan

Check the per-person minimum, order a manageable first round, and ask staff for help with the grill.

Before you order

  • Confirm prices, portions and payment methods before ordering.
  • Ask about allergens and cross-contact; recipes vary by vendor.
  • Check current hours and transport schedules; availability changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will staff cook the meat?
Service varies. Some restaurants help at the table; others expect diners to grill their own.
How should I handle allergies?
Tell staff about your allergy and ask about ingredients and cross-contact. Never rely on appearance alone.
How much should I budget?
Prices vary by shop and portion. Check the displayed menu and confirm the total before ordering.
Can I pay by card?
Many businesses accept cards, but policies vary. Carry some cash as a backup.
