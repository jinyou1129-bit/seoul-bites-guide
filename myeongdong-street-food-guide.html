Myeongdong Street Food Survival Guide

Kept in this browser only.

Published 2026-11-19

Myeongdong’s shopping streets offer a convenient introduction to snack-style eating; stalls and hours change.

What to try

  • Egg bread (계란빵): Warm, lightly sweet bread with an egg makes an easy snack.
  • Hotteok (호떡): Let the hot syrup cool before eating and check for nuts.
  • Tornado potato: A spiral-cut potato on a skewer is easy to share; seasoning may include dairy.
  • Grilled skewers (꼬치): Check the ingredients, price and sauce before ordering.

A simple plan

Walk the street once, compare prices, then share a couple of snacks. Dispose of skewers safely.

Before you order

  • Confirm prices, portions and payment methods before ordering.
  • Ask about allergens and cross-contact; recipes vary by vendor.
  • Check current hours and transport schedules; availability changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all stalls open every day?
No single schedule covers every vendor. Check current trading hours.
How should I handle allergies?
Tell staff about your allergy and ask about ingredients and cross-contact. Never rely on appearance alone.
How much should I budget?
Prices vary by shop and portion. Check the displayed menu and confirm the total before ordering.
Can I pay by card?
Many businesses accept cards, but policies vary. Carry some cash as a backup.
