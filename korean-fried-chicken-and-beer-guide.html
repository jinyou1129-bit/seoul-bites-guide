Chimaek Guide: Korean Fried Chicken and Beer

Kept in this browser only.

Published 2026-11-20

Chimaek combines chicken (치킨) and beer (맥주), usually over a relaxed shared meal.

What to try

  • Original crispy chicken: Ask whether it is bone-in or boneless and how large the portion is.
  • Yangnyeom chicken (양념치킨): Sweet-savory red sauce coats the chicken; heat levels vary.
  • Soy garlic chicken: A savory-sweet choice; the glaze may contain soy and wheat.
  • Pickled radish (치킨무): Cool, crunchy radish is a common accompaniment; check if it costs extra.

A simple plan

Share a chicken and order drinks to suit your group. Nonalcoholic drinks work too; never drink and drive.

Before you order

  • Confirm prices, portions and payment methods before ordering.
  • Ask about allergens and cross-contact; recipes vary by vendor.
  • Check current hours and transport schedules; availability changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to drink beer?
No. Soft drinks, water and nonalcoholic options are fine alternatives.
How should I handle allergies?
Tell staff about your allergy and ask about ingredients and cross-contact. Never rely on appearance alone.
How much should I budget?
Prices vary by shop and portion. Check the displayed menu and confirm the total before ordering.
Can I pay by card?
Many businesses accept cards, but policies vary. Carry some cash as a backup.
