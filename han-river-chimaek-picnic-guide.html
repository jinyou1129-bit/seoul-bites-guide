Han River Chimaek & Instant Ramen Guide: How to Picnic Like a Local

Chicken and beer (chimaek), instant ramen and a river view: here is a realistic plan for a relaxed Hangang picnic, including practical alternatives if you cannot use Korean delivery apps.

Pick a park and rent a mat

Yeouido Hangang Park: broad lawns and sunset views; start at Yeouinaru Station, Line 5. Nearby rental shops may offer a mat, low table and tent bundles. Ask the price, return time, deposit and whether setup is included before paying. Availability and opening hours vary by weather and season.

Banpo Hangang Park: choose the riverside near Banpo Bridge for the fountain and sunset. Search for nearby picnic-rental shops before arriving; do not assume a rental counter will be open inside the park. Carry your kit back before the agreed return time.

Tent rules and permitted areas can change: follow current park signs. Do not pitch a tent outside permitted zones or block paths; take rubbish away and leave the lawn clean.

How to cook convenience-store ramen

  1. Buy a dry instant ramen packet and the store's designated foil cooking tray. Confirm the machine and tray are available before paying; cup noodles are not necessarily suitable.
  2. Place the noodles and seasoning in the tray. Follow the machine's posted instructions for water: some dispense it automatically; others require you to add the marked amount.
  3. Put the foil tray flat on the designated cooker, select the ramen setting and press start. Use only the tray approved for that machine—not a regular metal bowl or a microwave.
  4. Wait until the cycle finishes; stir carefully. The tray, steam and broth are very hot. Ask staff for help if the instructions differ.
  5. Carry it by the edges with care, add toppings if you like, and dispose of the tray where indicated.

Chicken delivery without a Korean phone number

Park delivery zones (배달존) are designated pickup points, not delivery to your picnic blanket. Locate the zone and its number on the park map first and meet the rider there. A data-only eSIM does not give you a Korean voice number, and Korean apps may require a local number, verification or a supported payment method; do not enter a fake number.

Reliable alternatives: buy takeaway from a chicken shop near a station on your way, or ask your hotel concierge to place an order using their own contact details and explain the pickup arrangements. If a particular restaurant accepts an online order or guest ordering without a local number, confirm the pickup zone and contact method before paying; policies differ. Allow time for delays at busy sunset hours.

Sunset and the Banpo fountain

Yeouido has open views along the river; at Banpo, position yourself along the river with a clear view of Banpo Bridge. Arrive early for a comfortable spot. The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain is seasonal and may be cancelled for weather, maintenance or events. Times change by date, so check the Seoul city website for the current schedule before traveling; do not rely on an old timetable.

Sample budget per person

A simple shared picnic can be roughly ₩15,000–₩30,000 per person: chicken share ₩9,000–₩16,000, ramen and drink ₩4,000–₩7,000, shared basic mat rental ₩2,000–₩7,000. These are illustrative estimates, not quoted prices; tent upgrades, delivery fees and solo orders can cost more. Check current prices.

Before you go

Pack tissues and a rubbish bag; bring layers for evening wind. Alcohol rules and local notices may change. Avoid the riverside during flood alerts and heed park closures.
